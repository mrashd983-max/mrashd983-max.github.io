ARTY  Privacy Policy
Effective date: November 1, 2026  •  App: ARTY — Learn to Draw  •  Publisher: MOH (sole developer), Amman, Jordan  •  Contact: mrashd983@gmail.com

ARTY is a drawing app for children. We designed it to collect as little data as possible — in fact, almost none. This policy explains exactly what happens with information in the app, in plain language.

1. Who this app is for

ARTY is directed to children and families. We comply with the children's privacy principles of COPPA (US), the UK Children's Code, and GDPR (EU/UK) regarding children.

2. What ARTY does NOT collect

  • No accounts. The app asks only for a first name or nickname, stored only on your device.
  • No personal information. We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking. ARTY contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no cross-app tracking.
  • No advertising. ARTY has no ads and never sells data.
  • No voice or audio recording. Audio in the app is pre-made lesson music that plays on your device. The microphone is never used.
  • No internet uploads of children's content. Everything your child creates stays on your device.

3. Camera and photos (art journal)

ARTY can ask for permission to use the camera so your child can photograph their own paper drawing and save it in the in-app journal.

  • Photos are stored only on your device (in the app's private storage).
  • Photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else.
  • You can delete any photo or all app data at any time (app settings, or uninstall the app).
  • If you do not grant camera permission, everything else in the app works normally.

4. Subscriptions and payments

ARTY Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by the app store (Google Play or Apple App Store). Payment information is handled by the store, never by ARTY. We receive only the fact that your subscription is active, through the store's receipt verification. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your store account settings, not in the app.

5. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following locally on your device only: the child's display name, lesson progress, journal photos, and subscription status. Deleting the app or clearing its storage removes all of it permanently.

6. Your rights

Parents and guardians may review, delete, or refuse any further collection of their child's information. Since we hold no server-side data about your child, everything can be erased simply by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For any question, contact us at the address above and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change what data the app handles (for example, adding optional cloud backup in the future), we will update this policy before the feature is released and describe the change here. The current version always applies to the current app release.
